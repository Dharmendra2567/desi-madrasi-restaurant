Vanakkam, Hyderabad

A little piece of Madras, right here in Kondapur

Desi Madrasi began with a simple wish — that anyone in Hyderabad craving the food of a Tamil home should not have to wait for their next trip down south. So we brought the grinding stone, the cast-iron tawa, the brass tumblers and the recipes that taste like somebody’s amma made them.

From the first idli of the morning to the last ghee roast of the night, everything on your plate is made the slow way: batter fermented overnight, sambar powder roasted and pounded in our own kitchen, coconut freshly grated, and coffee brewed by the slow drip of a traditional filter.

  • Stone-ground idli & dosa batter
  • Cold-pressed gingelly & coconut oil
  • Fresh banana leaf meals every day
  • Decoction brewed through the day
  • Chettinad masalas pounded in-house
  • Warm, family-friendly seating

From Sunrise to Starlight

A day at Desi Madrasi

A South Indian kitchen keeps its own clock. Our menu changes with the hours, exactly the way it would in a home in Mylapore or Madurai.

7AM

Morning Tiffin

Pillowy idlis, crunchy medu vadas, ven pongal with black pepper and ghee, and the first dosas off the tawa.

12PM

Leaf Meals

Unlimited rice with sambar, rasam, kootu, poriyal, appalam, curd and the sweet of the day.

4PM

Kaapi & Tiffin

Kuzhi paniyaram, mirchi bajji, onion pakoda and a tumbler of frothy filter coffee for the evening pause.

7PM

Dinner Feast

Ghee roasts, parottas with Chettinad curries, Dindigul-style biryani and comforting curd rice to close.

The Art of Saapadu

How a banana leaf meal unfolds

There is an order and a reason to everything on the leaf. Here is what to expect when you sit down for lunch.

  1. Salt, pickle & a little sweetPlaced first on the upper-left corner of the leaf, exactly as tradition asks.
  2. Poriyal & kootuA dry stir-fry of seasonal vegetables and a gentle lentil-coconut stew along the top edge.
  3. Appalam & vadaiCrackling papad and a crisp vada — the crunch every good meal needs.
  4. Rice, paruppu & gheeThe first round of steaming rice with soft dal and a generous spoon of ghee.
  5. Sambar, then rasamThick tamarind-lentil sambar, followed by peppery rasam that warms and settles.
  6. Curd rice & payasamThe cooling finish, followed by a ladle of payasam and a ripe banana.

Our Five Promises

What we will never compromise on

“

Batter ground fresh, every single evening.

Urad dal and rice are soaked, stone-ground and left to ferment naturally overnight. No ready-mix, no shortcuts, no soda — just the gentle sourness and softness that only time can give an idli.

“

Spices roasted and pounded in our own kitchen.

Sambar podi, rasam podi, gunpowder and Chettinad masala are dry-roasted in small batches and ground in-house, so every curry carries the aroma of spices that were whole only days ago.

“

Coffee the slow way, never from a machine.

Our blend of coffee and chicory drips through traditional brass filters, fresh through the day. It is mixed with boiling milk and poured high to raise that famous froth.

“

A fresh banana leaf for every guest at lunch.

Leaves arrive every morning, are washed and trimmed by hand, and laid out just before you sit. It is greener, it is traditional, and the food simply tastes better on it.

“

Every guest is treated like family.

We believe in “Athithi Devo Bhava” — the guest is god. Second helpings are offered, not requested, and nobody leaves without being asked how the meal was.
