Where Desi meets Madrasi, right here in Kondapur

Desi Madrasi began with a simple idea — bring Hyderabad’s favourite bagara and dum biryani to the same table as the Madras-style biryani and sukka of a Tamil home. Two kitchens, one family, and recipes that taste like somebody’s amma made them.

From the first plate of bagara at lunch to the last combo of the night, everything is made the slow way: meat marinated for hours, masalas roasted and ground in our own kitchen, and every biryani cooked together with its rice so the flavour goes all the way through.

  • Desi & Madrasi biryani styles

Our Five Promises

What we will never compromise on

“

Bagara rice made fresh for every service.

Whole spices, mint and golden fried onions tempered into the rice in small batches through the day. No reheated rice, no shortcuts — just the aroma that makes bagara worth coming back for.

“

Spices roasted and pounded in our own kitchen.

Biryani masala, sukka masala and curry powders are dry-roasted in small batches and ground in-house, so every dish carries the aroma of spices that were whole only days ago.

“

Biryani cooked slowly, never rushed.

Meat is marinated for hours, then cooked together with the rice so every grain soaks up the masala. It takes longer, and you can taste the difference.

“

A fresh banana leaf for every guest.

Leaves arrive every morning, are washed and trimmed by hand, and laid out just before you sit. It is greener, it is traditional, and the food simply tastes better on it.

“

Every guest is treated like family.

We believe in “Athithi Devo Bhava” — the guest is god. Second helpings are offered, not requested, and nobody leaves without being asked how the meal was.
